Mingeun Choi

Ph.D. Student in Mechanical Engineering
Georgia Institute of Technology

CV · Email · Google Scholar · LinkedIn · GitHub

My research focuses on electrothermal modeling and thin-film thermal-property characterization for heterogeneously integrated electronics. I work with Professor Satish Kumar in the Micro Nano Devices and Systems Laboratory at Georgia Tech.

Previously, I served as an Instructor and Assistant Professor at the Republic of Korea Air Force Academy. I received my M.S. from Seoul National University and B.S. from Korea University, both in Mechanical Engineering. See my CV above for my full academic record.

Research

  • Electrothermal co-design

    Vertical power delivery and substrate-embedded microfluidic cooling.

  • Machine-learning-accelerated thermal modeling

    Temperature prediction from individual transistors to arrays.

  • Thin-film thermal characterization

    Machine-learning-enabled frequency-domain thermoreflectance inversion.
