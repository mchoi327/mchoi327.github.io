Mingeun Choi

Ph.D. Student in Mechanical Engineering
Georgia Institute of Technology

CV · Email · Google Scholar · LinkedIn · GitHub

I study heat transport in advanced microelectronics through thin-film thermal characterization and the development of numerical and machine-learning models. I work with Professor Satish Kumar in the Micro Nano Devices and Systems Laboratory at Georgia Tech.

Previously, I served as an Instructor and Assistant Professor at the Republic of Korea Air Force Academy. I received my M.S. from Seoul National University and B.S. from Korea University, both in Mechanical Engineering. See my CV above for my full academic record.

Research

  • Materials and Interfaces

    Depth-dependent thin-film thermal properties from frequency-domain thermoreflectance.

  • Devices and Circuits

    Transient temperature fields and thermal crosstalk in FinFET arrays.

  • Packages and Systems

    Vertical power delivery, substrate-embedded microfluidic cooling, and transient heat-transfer modeling.
