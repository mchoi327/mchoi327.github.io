Welcome

My research focuses on electrothermal modeling and thin-film thermal-property characterization for heterogeneously integrated electronics.

  • Electrothermal co-design of vertical power delivery and substrate-embedded microfluidic cooling.
  • Machine-learning-accelerated prediction of transistor temperature fields, from individual devices to arrays.
  • Machine-learning-enabled frequency-domain thermoreflectance inversion for thin-film thermal-property characterization.
