Mingeun to present at IEEE IEDM 2024

November 26, 2024

Mingeun Choi will present “Fast Prediction of Spatio-Temporal Temperature Profiles in FinFET Arrays via Numerical and Machine-Learning Approaches” at IEEE IEDM in San Francisco, December 7–11, 2024. The work develops numerical and machine-learning models for 14 nm bulk FinFET arrays, accounting for nanoscale thermal resistance and heat transfer between neighboring transistors. The presentation was highlighted in the Georgia Tech School of Electrical and Computer Engineering’s preview of the university’s contributions to the conference.
